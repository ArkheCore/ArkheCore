## About ARKHE

ARKHE is being developed as a creative IP company focused on the creation, protection and long-term development of original intellectual property.

Our work brings together **creative systems, technology, AI-assisted production, digital tools and original content** while preserving human oversight, authorship, ethical boundaries and long-term continuity.

## What guides us

- **Ethics first** — capability never comes before responsibility.
- **Purposeful innovation** — technology should strengthen creation, not replace judgment.
- **Human oversight** — critical decisions remain under human authority.
- **Continuity by design** — systems should remain understandable, resilient and maintainable over time.
- **Original IP** — we build identities, worlds, tools and creative assets intended to develop coherently over the long term.

## Current stage

ARKHE is currently in its **foundational development phase**.

This GitHub account will serve as a technical home for selected public repositories, documentation and tools. Internal systems, unreleased intellectual property and experimental work will remain private until they are ready for publication.
